# Essential Eight regular-backup mapping

Authority: [ASD Essential Eight maturity model](https://www.cyber.gov.au/resources-business-and-government/essential-cyber-security/essential-eight/essential-eight-maturity-model). Confirm the assessment edition and its complete regular-backup criteria before scoring. The rows below organise relevant evidence by assessment level; they do not reproduce the normative control wording or establish that one level is achieved.

| Assessment level | Evidence to evaluate | Bunker feature and test | Residual / outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maturity Level 1 | Important data/configuration coverage, backup frequency/retention matched to recovery needs, and demonstrated restoration | [Policies](../user/policies.md), [restore procedure](../user/restore.md), [encrypted snapshot/restore and exclusion tests](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/crates/bunker/tests/repository.rs), [runbook smoke](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/packaging/scripts/smoke_restore.py) | Local restoration exercised. Full home-agent/Git coverage, automatic retention, independent recovery exercises and application readiness are incomplete. Level not demonstrated. |
| Maturity Level 2 | All applicable level criteria, including access restrictions and protection of backup data against unauthorised modification/deletion | [Security boundaries](../user/security.md), [MCP mutation restrictions and stale-approval tests](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/crates/bunker/tests/repository.rs), [tenant/role API tests](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/control-plane/tests/api.test.ts) | API restrictions do not stop an agent with same-user filesystem credentials. Production mTLS and independently enforced protected storage are absent. Level not demonstrated. |
| Maturity Level 3 | All applicable level criteria, including stronger privileged-access/retention protection, resilient recovery copies and demonstrated recoverability | [Replication guide](../user/storage.md), [encrypted transport and unproven-lock rejection tests](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/crates/bunker/src/storage.rs), [recovery exercise](https://github.com/jusso-dev/bunker/blob/main/packaging/scripts/smoke_restore.py) | No verified immutable copy, independent backup administration, scheduled full agent/MCP restoration or provider failure exercise. Level not demonstrated. |

Thematic allocation above is not a claim that any protection begins only at a particular level. Assess every required criterion in the selected publication; controls and maturity expectations can change. Preserve the assessor's criterion-by-criterion mapping alongside this implementation map.

Bunker's scope also does not implement the other seven Essential Eight strategies. Application control, patching, MFA and administrative privilege controls are environmental responsibilities. A working backup feature cannot establish overall maturity.

Before claiming a level, document recovery objectives, scope inventory and exclusions, measured backup/restore performance, retention and access settings, evidence of restore exercises, alert response, and acceptance of unresolved gaps. Obtain a responsible assessor's review; no such approval is recorded here.
